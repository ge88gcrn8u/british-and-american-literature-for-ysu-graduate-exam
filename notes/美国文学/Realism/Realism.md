1) **Point of view 视角** 亨利詹姆斯将其**理论化**

   Point of view is the angle of considering things,看问题的角度 which shows us the opinion or feelings of the individuals involved in a situation.展示了参与情况的个人的意见或感受。 In literature, point of view is the **mode of narration**叙事方式 that an author employs to\*\* let the readers "hear" and "see" what takes place in a story, poem, or essay.\*\*  让读者可以听到/ 观察到故事发生了什么

   Point of view is a reflection of\*\* the opinion an individual from real life or fiction has.\*\*

   Examples of point of view belong to one of these three major kinds:

   **First person point of view involves the use of either of the two pronouns "I" or "we."**

   "I felt like I was getting drowned with shame and disgrace." 惠特曼 song of myself

   **Second person point of view employs the pronoun "you."**

   "Sometimes you cannot clearly discern between anger and frustration." 一个陌生女人的来信 斯蒂芬·茨威格

   **Third person point of view uses pronouns like "he," "she,"" "it," "they," or a name.**

   Stewart is a principled man. He acts by the book and never lets you deceive him easily." 以一个客观的视角来看——马克吐温 哈克贝利费嗯历险记
2) Psychological realism

EX: Psychological realism is a style of writing that came to prominence in the late **19th and early 20th centuries.** It's a highly **character-driven genre of fiction writing角色驱动型,** as it focuses on the **motivations and internal thoughts of characters to explain their actions**. 用角色的动机和想法解释他的行为

: A writer of psychological realism seeks to \*\*show not only what the characters do but also to explain why they take such actions.**不仅是做了什么,而且是为什么做There's often a larger theme in psychological realism novels, with the author expressing an opinion o**n a societal or political issue \*\*通过他的角色描写社会和政治上的问题through his or her characters.

However, psychological realism should not be confused with\*\* psychoanalytic writing or surrealism\*\*,与心理分析和超现实主义区分开 two other modes of artistic expression that flourished in the 20th century and focused on psychology in unique ways. 但是心理现实主义对他们有促进作用
