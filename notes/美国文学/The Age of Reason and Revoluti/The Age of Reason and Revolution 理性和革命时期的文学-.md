# The Age of Reason and Revolution 理性和革命时期的文学&#x20;

# 背景 一般重要

1. 和清教主义时代的联系：**Theology** dominated American literature during **puritan period**, and **politics** became the core subject in this time. 神学曾经是清教主义时期的主要题材，这个时期政治是主题
2. 经济发展，国家观念出现：In the middle of 18thC, United States rapidly grows in **economy and population**. "**States**" began to replaced "**Colony**" in people's minds. 18世纪中叶 美国快速发展，states 取代了colony在人们的心中
3. 所以爆发了独立战争：The War of Independence (1776-1783) creates **the United States of America**独立战争创造了美国 （Freedom was also won by the fiery **rhetoric** of Thomas Paine's Common Sense and the **eloquence** of the Declaration of Independence.—**G. Washington**）
4. 美国启蒙运动（反对英国殖民，宗教蒙蔽主义和清教，提倡世俗，英国）：**American  Enlightenment** opposed **old colonial order** and **religious obscurantism** 美国启蒙运动反对旧殖民地的秩序和宗教蒙蔽主义，with **a fatal blow to Puritanism** 对清教致命一击, brought **secular education and literature** to life 让世俗教育和文学发展（富兰克林) Americans are interested to English  科学家 作家和 哲学家（牛顿，Swift, Locke等）法国的作家（尤其是伏尔泰Voltaire）它们的作品give 美国独立战争 **American thought and politics** **big influence**
5. 文学特点：美国的文学imitated 英国 18th C literature (e.g., Neoclassicism)，但是 brave Americans created **pamphlets and newspapers with great Ambition** 勇敢的美国人写小册子和报纸
   1. The general magazine 第一部殖民杂志——富兰克林
   2. Common Sense 小册子——托马斯潘恩

## Philip Freneau 飞利浦 福瑞诺— most prominent poet and political journalist after revolution 后革命时代最著名的诗人和新闻记者

1. 名誉
   1. **Father** of American Poetry 美国诗歌之父
   2. Poet of \*\*American Revolution \*\*美国革命诗人
   3. representative of **American literary nationalism** 美国文学民族主义的代表
   4. an important figure in the transition from **neoclassicism to romanticism** 新古典主义到浪漫主义的中间人物
   5. He heralded the **independence of American literature** 预示了美国文学的独立
      1. For his careful \*\*observation to nature \*\*and **his themes about native wild life** different from other native writers 他的自然的细致观察和与本土作家与众不同的对野生生活的处理
      2. 这也是他的**浪漫主义元素**
2. 主题
   1. 自然
   2. He devoted his poetry creation for independence. 写诗为了独立
      1. **praise** American's\*\* righteous cause\*\* 夸赞人们的正义
      2. expose the\*\* atrocities of British colonies\*\* 揭露英国人的暴行
3. 作品
   1. The Wild Honey Suckle 隐忍冬花 **ababcc** **4 stanzas; Iambic Tetrameter 四部抑扬格 8音节4音步**
      1. 美国的**超验主义** 的 **seed**
      2. 写了**美国的 subject **美国国土的题材 ，抛弃**（cast off）了英国的style**
      3. 主题
         1. Time is **transient** and death is **inevitable**, but **nature** is **beautiful and lovely** 时间稍纵即逝 死亡在所难免 但是自然是美丽的
         2. He loves **nature** and fell **sorry to shortness of life** 热爱自然，对生命的短暂惋惜
         3. 但是诗人是**positive**，因为**life and death are natural** 生死是自然的
      4. 内容

         The speaker in [Philip Freneau](https://owlcation.com/humanities/Life-Sketch-of-Philip-Freneau-Father-of-American-Poetry "Philip Freneau")'s "The Wild Honeysuckle" addresses a lovely honeysuckle flower, marveling in its beauty and the surroundings in which he finds the flowers; then he turns to philosophizing about the nature of the little flower and how its situation mirrors the lives beings. 菲利普·弗雷诺（Philip Freneau）的《野生金银花》中的演讲者对一朵可爱的金银花说，惊叹于它的美丽和他发现花朵的环境;然后，他转向哲学思考小花的本质，以及它的情况如何反映所有物的生活。
   2. The Indian Burying Ground 印第安人的墓地**abab**
      1. This poem is about Indian funeral, which is very different from the white's. The dead Indian is dressed **in special cloth and buried in a sitting posture as if celebrating** **a happy event.** 死人穿着特殊的衣服 坐着的姿势 好像庆祝着开心的事情Death in the Indian tradition has a different meaning. **It is no longer that sad and dreadful event that everyone is afraid of. The dead person becomes a warrior to protect the land.** 死亡不再恐惧，死者是守护土地的勇士
   3. To a caty-did 致纺织娘
      1. "Caty-did" is the sound of a well-known insect.是一种虫子的叫声
      2. This poem reveals the poet's love for nature and his keen observation of living things in nature; it also shows the mystery of nature. 揭示了作者对自然的爱，和对自然中生物热切的观察，也象征大自然的神秘
