# Romanticism 也叫 American Renaissance (end of 18thC- civil war, the publication of Irving's The Sketch Book to Whitman's Leaves of Grass)

# 背景 一般重要

1. 政治：US experienced the **population growth, westward movement and abolition of voting restrictions**. **All white men are created equal** prevailed. 人口增长，西进运动，废除投票限制政策。白人生来平等观念盛行
2. 经济：**By 1860s**, America turned into **industrial and urbanized society** by industrial revolution, but **the gap** between rich and poor **increased**. 1860年左右，因为工业革命，美国进入了工业化和城镇化社会/ 但是贫富差距增大
   1. NY, the largest city, became the center of **culture and economy.** 作为美国最大的城市，纽约成为了经济和文化之都。
3. 文化教育：By **1850s**, **literacy rate** improved , and American native writers had good **reputation** **domestically and internationally**. 美国识字率提升，美国作家在国内外声誉良好
4. 社会运动：**abolishment and feminist **movements were flourished in America, seeking** reform, freedom and fairness**. 废奴运动和女性主义兴起，寻求改革，自由和平等。
   1. **Transcendentalist** and **baptists**, and so forth found a new way to believe in god. 超验主义 浸礼教 寻找信仰上帝的新方式
