# Jack London 自然主义，少关注 style，暴力可以检验人性，权力意志和生存法则 马丁伊登——社会 越来越孤独阶级 野性的呼唤——反对人类中心

Jack London is representative of American realism, a more negatively realism, that is naturalism, and his work "Martin Eden " and " The Call of Wild " makes him immortal in American literature.

Naturalism is a more negative realism, under the influence of Social Darwinism, pessimism and determinism, and It believes that the destiny of a man is determined by many factors including environment, economy, or inherence, and men cannot control their life and achieve their dreams. The characters they concerns are from lower class. and the writers employs more negative and satire languages in their works.  The representatives of Naturalism are jack London and Theodore Dreiser.

Jack London less concerns his language and style, which is **less refined, more crude and simple**. His theme is mainly about the conflict between weak power and strong power, and **violence, in his work, can test human character.** **The law of survival and power are competing in his novel.**

Martin Eden Is one of his work. It's about the main character Martin Eden, a sailor, who loves a girl, **Morse** whose brother has been helped by him. He works hard and becomes a rich literati , but he finds his life becomes more darker and lonelier, finally, he suicides. **The theme of this work is social class. as Martin Eden becomes more and more wiser, he finds that his distance between working class, and capitalism becomes much more longer, and he becomes more isolated.**

**The Call of the Wild criticizes “human-central”. **The story cares A domestic dog callsBuck recovers his wildness, and comes back to nature. The core of animal life is** a conflict and survival of the strongest.**

In all, as a symbol of naturalism, Jack London by his exciting and cruel style, profoundly reveals the darkness at his time.
