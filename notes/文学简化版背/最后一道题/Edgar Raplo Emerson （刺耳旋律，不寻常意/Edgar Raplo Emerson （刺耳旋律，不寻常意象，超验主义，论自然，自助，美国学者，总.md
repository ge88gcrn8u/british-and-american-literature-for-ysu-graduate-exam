# Edgar Raplo Emerson （刺耳旋律，不寻常意象，超验主义，论自然，自助，美国学者，总结——时代的 prophet）

Emerson is the one of the most representative writers in American Romanticism  and the **most outstanding spokesman of** **transcendentalism**. His **harsh rhythm, unusual images are praised. 刺耳的韵律 不寻常的意象**

As the climax of American Romanticism, transcendentalism **(1830s- civil war, New England)** is the doctrine by transcendentalists represented by Emerson and Thoreau, and Emerson's Nature is the bible of transcendentalism. The aim of transcendentalism is **against the American materialism**.  The theory originates the **Eastern Mysticism and European Romanticism**. It puts intuition, individual and feeling over reason and ration.

As aforementioned, **Nature** is the **Bible** of the transcendentalism. **The theme cares the importance of nature to human**.  Nature introduces 3 groups of the concepts of transcendentalism, including oversoul, individual and **nature, which is the symbol of god .** Oversoul is the core of the transcendentalism, which is an all-pervading power that of goodness, contains all things and the sources of all things. Individual can exchange with divine, so people are divines. The world is made for men, and the most ideal type of people is of self-reliance, which **fights against the non-humanity of puritanism**. Nature is the symbol of goodness, which can adjust the relation of men and god, and has a restorative effect on health, so \*\*the world is symbolic. \*\*

孩子看见Nature also mentions only child can enjoy the nature from the bottom of their hearts, that **the sun shines into the eye and heart of child**.  太阳射进孩子的心里，孩子才能感受

眼球Emerson also converts an transparent eye ball that when one feels completely **merged** in the outside, and nature. The soul will become , and transcend the limits of individual. 变成眼球因为当人们完全沉浸在自然，就会超过一切限制，soul 变成了 oversoul

His\*\* American Scholar\*\* is the\*\* intellectual declaration of independence \*\*美国智慧独立宣言, which calls scholars to **be original in literature creation, engage in society and cast off  Europe influences** 原创，参与社会，摆脱欧洲影响

His **Self-Reliance** calls people should be **self-reliant that people can truly independent.** 自立才能独立 This work has great impact\*\* on Thoreau's idea.\*\*

In sum, As the **prophet** \*\* at his time 他的时代的先知, \*\*he has great influence on Thoreau, Hawthorne and Whitman, and one of the best philosophers until present.
