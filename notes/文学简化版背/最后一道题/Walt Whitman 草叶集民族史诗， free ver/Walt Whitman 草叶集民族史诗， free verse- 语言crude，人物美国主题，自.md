As a great innovator of American Poetry, What Whitman employs free verse in his poems. He is the representative figure between romanticism and realism. His\*\* ****Leaves of Grass, the American national epic**** **symbolizes the end of American romanticism. He concerns the** en-masses \*\*of America.

**Free verse** is his major feature of poetry. It is originated from France.\*\* Instead of traditional iambic pentameter or other rhyme schemes\*\*, there is no regular beat and rhyme in his works, instead of phonetic recurrence and parallelism at beginning of lines.  Free verse also influences Emily Dickson and other other 20th American literature poets; **Pond**.

**Song of Myself **is the first poem in Leaves of Grass. It creates a **world** without social classes and radically equal.He praises the** nationalism and democracy, individualism and humanity**. He keeps **poem** **beliefs of singularity and equality.** He uses symbolisms in this poem. You symbolizes **en-mass,** and I is not **a particular person**. Whitman**free himself** and **convey his individualism and nationalism **by** combining the first and second person perspective**

**O Captain! My Captain!** is his poem memorizes the president Lincoln died in 1865. \*\* 他夸赞林肯的地位，改变了这个国家\*\*O Captain! My Captain!”, which indicates his deep love and respect for the Captain. In the second stanza, the speaker speaks to “you”, which shows his difficulty in coming to terms with his Captain’s death, while in the third stanza, the speaker has finally accepted that his Captain is dead, so he in no way addresses his Captain directly but speaks of him entirely in the third-person.
