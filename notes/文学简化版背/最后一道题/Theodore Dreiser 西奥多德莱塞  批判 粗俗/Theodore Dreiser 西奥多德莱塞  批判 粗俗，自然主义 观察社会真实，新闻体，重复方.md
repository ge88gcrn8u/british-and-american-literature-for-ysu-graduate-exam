# Theodore Dreiser 西奥多德莱塞  批判 粗俗，自然主义 观察社会真实，新闻体，重复方式，印象，美国梦的幻灭，物质主义，性，新女性

As a representative of naturalism, a more negative realism, Theodore Dreiser suffers deluges criticisms at his time. Some says his work is **amoral**, **pornographic or more vulgar**, and others says his style is dull and his language is **awkward**. Actually, his work reflects his society truly, especially Sister Carrie.&#x20;

Naturalism is a more negative realism, under the influence of Social Darwinism, pessimism and determinism, and It believes that the destiny of a man is determined by many factors including environment, economy, or inherence, and men cannot control their life and achieve their dreams. The characters they concerns are from lower class. and the writers employs more negative and satire languages in their works.  The representatives of Naturalism are jack London and Theodore Dreiser.

Theodore is a \*\*good observer \*\*of his society. By his observation, his work is more **convincing** and more **real**. By employment of **journalistic style**, his **reiteration** leaves great impression to readers. His characters, is under control of environment ,**inherence** that **they cannot achieve their dreams.**

Sister Carrie is his representative work. It tells a country girl comes to **Chicago for her American Dream,** which symbolizes the **disillusionment of American dream** 美国梦的破灭 He criticizes the **materialism**, and **sex** is the only way to climb the social class. Carrie is a\*\* new woman\*\* that she is **independent**  of economy and she has many chances for sex, but also **,her life symbolizes naturalism**. The \*\* rocking chair\*\* in this novel, symbolizes **uncertainty** of life.

Theodore Dreiser, as a social criticizer, leads us to observe his society and reflects true America for us.
