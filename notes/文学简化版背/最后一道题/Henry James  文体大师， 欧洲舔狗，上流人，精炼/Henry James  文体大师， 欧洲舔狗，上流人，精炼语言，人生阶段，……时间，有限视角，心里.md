# Henry James  文体大师， 欧洲舔狗，上流人，精炼语言，人生阶段，……时间，有限视角，心里现实主义，黛西米勒， 贵妇人画像，意识流，艺术联系生活（小说反应生活），从浪漫和传奇到分析角色面对社会

Henry James is the representative of realism, and an\*\* admirer of Europe. **Contrast to Mark Twain and William Dean Howell, his characters are basically from**higher class \*\*. and by**his refined language that he is a great stylist,** his international theme, psychological realism and limit points of view make him well-known.

There are 3 stages of his creation. The first period he mainly concerns the **international theme**, which is about the communication between naive America and mature Europe, **which symbolizes 2 different values**. (Daisy Miller)America gains growth by the **process** Marriage and love connects tightly to international theme，and Daisy Miller is the representative of international theme. The second \*\* explores more subtle personal characters\*\*, and **relationships**. And the last period, he turns to the **novella** and\*\* international theme\*\*, the most mature period.(The Ambassadors" (1903))

**Limit points of view** is his technique to narrative story. He rejected the **omniscient** point of view to tell his story, but use limitedly, which aims to reveal the characters themselves by **minimal intervention**. In The Portrait of Lady, he uses **Isabel** point's of view to see the world

**Psychological realism** is another most important\*\* technique of explore\*\* the mental state of characters. It aims to introduce motivation and thought of characters and he stresses that **psychological perception is the best way of Psychological realism.**

**Daisy Miller **is one of his best work. By the narration of a** American man  Winterbourne**. It introduces an American girl killed in Rome, and it is his **international theme for the first time**. Daisy Miller, is  the symbolization of **freedom and innocence** because of\*\* his relationship between men\*\*, but she is actually kind.  **Her innocence of her actions lead to misunderstandings in Europe.**&#x20;

**The Portait of a Lady **is also a work of International theme. The novel tells about an American girl**, Isabel Archer**, who arrived in Europe and wanted to live **a free and noble life**, but was hurt **by Madame Merle and Gilbert Osmon**d. Isabel's dream was broken and she did not aware of that, finally, she died. The theme concerns the anxieties of the modern world 对现代社会的焦虑 and **social rituals, isolation, and love** **without response** 社会礼仪，孤立和不被回应的爱

As a master of **stream of consciousness and modernism**, Henry James believes that **art related to life and novel represents life**. He turns novel from **journalism** and **romance** to **analyze** **individuals facing to society**. His ideas and work has great influence \*\*on future generations. \*\*
