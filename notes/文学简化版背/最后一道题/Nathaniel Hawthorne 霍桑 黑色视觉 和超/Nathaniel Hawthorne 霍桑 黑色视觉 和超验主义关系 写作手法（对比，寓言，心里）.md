# Nathaniel Hawthorne 霍桑 黑色视觉 和超验主义关系 写作手法（对比，寓言，心里），红字（主题，成长小说Bildungsroman，sin ，矛盾，A，麦尔维尔评价）

As a friend and teacher of Melville, Nathaniel Hawthorne is one of the most important representatives of American Romanticism, and he becomes famous for his black vision, his basic idea.

Black vision is affected by puritanism, especially for its total depravity, **predestination** and original sin. Since his ancestors has so-called unforgiven sins, Hawthorne believes that everyone has sin, which can inherited from generation to generation, and sin will finally be punished. **（Young Goodman Brown and The House of the Seven Gables）** However, sin can also educate people, and makes people better. (The Scarlet Letter)&#x20;

Also, he is also under influence of transcendentalism. He opposes the positive attitudes to human and\*\* human cain gain progress\*\* as transcendentalists affirm and the effect that nature can exert on men. Surely, their ideas has samenesses. They both deny science.

His literary ideas are also predominant. He believes that **romance** is the only way to account American history. **Contradiction**对比 thought is **his prédominant feature**. He is a master of **symbolism**, especially **ambiguity** to criticize society.(A) **Allegory** is also his **weapon** to **criticize** society. Finally, he is good at explore people's psychology that his works care **many psychological activities of characters**.

The Scarlet Letter is most important work, which talks about **Hester**, under the sin of adultery, changes herself step by step in her town. He debates the **si**n's universal, inherent, punishment and educational effect on human by \*\*Hester and Pearl.  \*\*and  It is a **Bildungsroman**, that describe the mental growth of Hester. He shows 2 pairs of **contractions** in this work that are **between society and individual, and between facing choices.**

**Symbolism** exerts great effect in this work, especially 3 times appearance of Letter “**A**”, that are **adultery (her crime), able (her efforts in town), angel (her relation with citizens gains the true meaning of her life)**

In all, as a master of symbolism and his creative literary ideas, Hawthorne's achievement is immortal. Melville called him **The largest brain with the largest heart 心胸最伟大 宽广的智慧**
