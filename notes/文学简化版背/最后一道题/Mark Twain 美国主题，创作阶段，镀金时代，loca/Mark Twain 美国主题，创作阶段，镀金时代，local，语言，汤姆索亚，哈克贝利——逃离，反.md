As the true father of American literature, Mark Twain is a representative of realism **.** \*\* He mainly cares the lower class Americans, and writes American subject\*\*. His carrer includes 3 periods, from humor to humor + critic to pure critic

**Gilded Age** is one of his works, and also symbolizes the society with great gap between poor and rich at that time, which is of **extremes and excess / poverty and wealth/ decline and progress, , of gloom and hope**

**Local Colorism s**tarts during 1860s to the end of 19th century, proposed by\*\* Brant Harte \*\* and its greatest representative is Mark Twain. It uses the \*\*local dialects, slangs \*\* to romanticize and describe the view of the American countryside manners and customs, especially Mississipi River. The Adventures of Huckleberry Finnsis the most important work of Twain.

Also, he used **colloquialism** to make his words to depict his characters more vivid. **He uses vernacular, and correct words. Colloquialism has great effect on Hemingway.**

The Adventures of Tom Sawyer is one of his works.It is a story about Tom Sawyer and Huckleberry Finn. It criticizes the adulthood, religion belief and education system, but it does not talks more.

The Adventures of Huckleberry is his greatest work of local colorism, as Hemingway mentioned the **modern America literature starts from this book**. It is a book about the adventure between Huck and Jim, a slave. The theme is **escape** that Huck wants to escape from **civilization** and Jim wants to escape from **slavery system**.  It is a **anti-slavery book**, and it introduces the westward movement and american. Although it romanticizes that period, it criticizes the slavery system. **Huck is original voice of american. He is common, honesty ,and his world transcends the time.**

As a great humorist, with his work Huckleberry Finn and Tom Sawyer, and his ideas of gilided age, colloquialism and local colorism, Mark Twain is immortal in worldwide literature.

In this novel, the friendship between Huck and Jim is impressive because it violates the convention that most Whites don't consider the Black to be their equal. At first Huck doesn't trust Jim until Jim helps him. Huck thinks that Jim is as good as whites, even much better in some aspects, so he helps Jim to get freedom. Their friendship trumps the racial discrimination.
