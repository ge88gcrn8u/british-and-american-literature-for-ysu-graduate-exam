As a representative of Imagists, T. S. Eliot has made great contribution to  poetic critics,  for example, new criticism. His impersonal theory has revive the 17th England\*\* metaphysical poems and French Symbolisms\*\*. Also, he is a representative of **lost generation, revealing the meaningless western world.**

Imagism is a branch of modernism in poetry, which stresses the fragmentation and juxtaposition, and works should be direct to subject. It starts around 1910s, and representative by Ezra Pond, T.S.Eliot. They advocate the economy of language and image, and one image is the best to create a poem. They use musical phrase rather than metrobone. T.S. Eliot's The Waste Land is the representative of this trend.

（lost generation）

For his artistic theory, his impersonal theory shocks the literaties. He advocates that the poem critics should be\*\* non-humanized\*\*, that is never consider the\*\* extra elements\*\* for the poem, for example, the background of the writer. Poem should be analyzed by intellect, feeling and experiences, which develops the inner analysis. He believes that poems are organism which constitutes by imagination and experience, his objective correlative is the only way to express emotions. He comments on the 17th metaphysical poem and 19th French symbolisms that ignites them again. In all ,Eliot is a leader of the new criticism from 1920s  to 1960s.

He uses no\*\* fixed beat or rhyme\*\* in his poems, and symbolism, **allusion  juxtaposition and fragmentation** are used in his poems. Like Canto of Pond, he aims to reveal the\*\* meaningless western world\*\*

The Love song of J.Alfred Prufrock is his representative. The theme cares the old traditions influence make people living in other's opinions.On the way to confess his love, Prufrock find his disadvantages and admit his coward. Prufrock is the reflection of the 20th intellectuals that was heavily influenced by the old traditions, not confident, and pessimistic.  Satire is a method he uses. The title has a love but the poem has no love, and his name is also a contradiction that Alfred is a noble name and Prufrock is a common name, the biggest satire is the he cannot his life in vain. Also, he uses dramatic monologue, the stream of consciousness and images which **defamilarizes things to ocean, that shows his distance to the world.** Also, **allusion** is what he used, such as\*\* excerpts of Bible or Hamlet.\*\*

The Waste Land is a symbolism of\*\* Lost Generation\*\*, which introduces the emptiness of sex failure that shows the meaningless of western world, and the **disillusionment** of the post WWI generation.
