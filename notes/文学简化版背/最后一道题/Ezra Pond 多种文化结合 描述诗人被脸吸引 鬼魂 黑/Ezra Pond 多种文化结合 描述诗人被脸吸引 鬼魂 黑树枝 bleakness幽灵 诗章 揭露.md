As a political controversial figure, Ezra Pond is the representative of **imagism**, and his literary ideas made him immortal in poetry. Also, by **integration between cultures, French symbolism, renaissance, traditional culture, Chinese culture, etc.**, he enhances the popularity of Chinese culture in Europe.&#x20;

Imagism is a branch of modernism in poetry, which stresses the fragmentation and juxtaposition, and works should be direct to subject. It starts around 1910s, and representative by Ezra Pond, T.S.Eliot. They advocate the economy of language and image, and one image is the best to create a poem. They use musical phrase rather than metrobone.  Pond's In a Station of Metro is the greatest work of the trend.

In a Station of Metro is the representative work of Pond. He makes full uses of Japanese Haiku with 14 words, 8 words  in the first line and 6 words in the second line , like\*\*  sonnet\*\*. The language is simple and economy, and uses non-traditional musical form.  It demonstrate**s the narrator is attracted by the beauty of  women and children from the dim station, so Pond decides to writes these dark faces.** The two biggest images are **petals and black boughs**, which is **a metaphor**. Petals, **a plosive vowel /p/，** represents the beauty of\*\* ladies face \*\*and **black bough** represents **bleakness** 凄凉. **Aspiration** in this poem represents the **fast modern life**, that Pond does not look these faces clearly.

Canto is his\*\* intellectual diary **in 1915. He uses many languages and cultures to see the** harmony and order of wisdom\*\* of Confucius, not the **gloom** of **western culture**. It shows a\*\* meaningless world\*\*, that Pond should give the **meaning** to the world by **art**. **Usury** is also an important theme, that is is a **monster** that ruins life.

For his imagism, his two works \`In a station of Metro\` and the cantos, Pond's poem leaves great influence to the world&#x20;
