# Herman Melville（海洋小说，象征，消极，没有上帝，超验主义，莫比迪克， 写作手法，主题 rejection and quest alienation，象征船，航程，Ahab，莫比迪克和他的白色Ishmeal，泰比，20 世纪火）

As the closest friend of Hawthorne, Herman Melville is one of the most important representatives in Romanticism Period. His\*\* sea adventure\*\* stories make him immortal.

He is also a master of **symbolism**(ambiguity) and his Moby-Dick is the creative peak of symbolism.&#x20;

\*\*He expresses his pessimistic view to the world that there is no god and no faith in the world 没有上帝that makes his ideas illuminated in 20th century.  \*\*

His attitude to **transcendentalism** is also negative. He opposes the \*\*individualism and oversoul \*\*by the help of his depiction of Ahab, whose death has directly influenced by his individualism.

Moby-Dick is his greatest work, which is called the **first prose epic of America** and **Shakespearean tradegy**. By the\*\* third person narrator Ishamal **and**multiple perspectives \*\*, he introduces the story of captain  Ahab who revenge to a whale, Moby-Dick and the panorama on the ship,**Pequod**.  Finally, Ahab still fails, and only Ishmael survives.&#x20;

One of the theme of Moby Dick is **alienation**between men, men and nature and men and society.  The other Is rejection and quest.  For rejection **, Ahab's pride rejected** by the whale (attacked), so he has to revenge. Ahab also rejects his**crews** that he discards their emotion and help, then comes to the **abyss of his obsession.** 欲望的深渊 A **black Pip**, was rejected that represent **the racial discrimination of the whole society**

On the ship, they all quest for something. Certainly, Ahab quests for the **whale, Moby-Dick itself.** The crew quests for **wealth**. Only Ishmael, quests for **knowledge and truth** 知识和自我探索

As aforementioned, Moby Dick is the **peak** of **symbolism**. the whole **voyage**  is for\*\* search and discovery for truth\*\*. **The Pequod is the little panorama and encyclopedia of society.** The Moby-Dick is **both evil and goodness **of nature. It is **ultimate mystery of universe**. 宇宙的最终奥秘 The color of Moby Dick is of** Self-Contradiction** that it symbolizes both **death and purity.** **Ahab** is the symbolization of **strong  individualism.** Ishmael is the\*\* explorer of the meaningless life\*\*.

**Typee is his his adventures in pacific ocean and a romantic account of savages**
