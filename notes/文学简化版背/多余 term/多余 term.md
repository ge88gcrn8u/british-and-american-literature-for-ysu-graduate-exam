[A foil character is a character who contrasts with another character, to  ](https://www.storyboardthat.com/articles/e/foil-character "A foil character is a character who contrasts with another character, to  ")[**emphasize certain qualitie**](https://www.storyboardthat.com/articles/e/foil-character "emphasize certain qualitie")[s of the main character](https://www.storyboardthat.com/articles/e/foil-character "s of the main character")  一个对比其他角色，来衬托主角

Foil characters can be friends, enemies, or even minor characters who have little role in the plot. 可以是朋友 敌人小角色

&#x20;It is a feeling **dangerous** to happen, and readers concern **characters** that  authors use to \*\*keep their readers’ interested \*\*throughout the work. 感觉到危险来临，**读者关心人物**，作者让读者 感兴趣

allows the writer to establish **the time, location, and environmen**t in which story takes place 时间，地点，环境 故事发生的

author use this  to create situations in which the [**audience**](https://literarydevices.net/audience/ "audience") knows **more** about the conflict and resolution **than** the  characters  创造读者比角色知道的更多的情景

lyric / Lyric poems 抒情诗

A lyric uses **a single **[**speaker**](https://literarydevices.net/speaker/ "speaker"), in** first person**， who expresses personal **emotions** .  一个说话者，表达个人情感，通过第一人称视角

personification

a common form of \*\*metaphor \*\*in that **human characteristics** are attributed to **nonhuman things**. 一种暗喻，人的特征在非人类事物上

特点：**juxtaposition  and fragmentation** 破碎和移位 / non-linear structure 非线性结构/多重叙事角度 multiple narration perspectives/意识流，心理探索
